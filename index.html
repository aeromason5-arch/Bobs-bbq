Our Story

Bob's BBQ started in 1985 when Bob Thompson decided to turn his backyard passion into a full-time pursuit. What began as weekend cookouts for friends and family has grown into a beloved local institution.

For nearly 40 years, we've been serving authentic, slow-smoked barbecue made the old-fashioned way. We wake up before dawn to tend our smokers, using only seasoned oak and hickory wood to create that perfect smoke ring and bark.

Every sauce, rub, and side dish is made from scratch using recipes passed down through three generations of the Thompson family. We believe great BBQ can't be rushed, and neither can we.

- Bob Thompson, Pitmaster
